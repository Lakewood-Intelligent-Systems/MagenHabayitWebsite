Good to know

Giving, made clear.

How does my donation help?

Magen Habayit covers 50% of eligible repair costs, while the family covers the remaining 50%. Support is available as long as funds remain in the nonprofit account.

Does this website collect payment details?

No. This website directs you to Zelle; your bank handles your payment. Never enter banking passwords or card details on this website. Confirm the recipient name before sending money.

Which repairs does the program support?

Necessary repairs and related expenses that help keep a home safe and functional, including plumbing, electrical, doors and locks, drywall, carpentry, fixtures, and urgent maintenance.
